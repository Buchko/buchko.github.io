What I Do in my Free Time

Productive Hobbies

Self Improvement

I enjoy trying to better myself. In doing this, I meditate, listen to Buddhist podcasts, research, and make goals for the future.

Speech and Debate

I like to compete in Speech and Debate as it forces me to better my social skills. I’m currently preparing to compete in impromptu speaking in the upcoming state tournament.

Playing Piano

I play the piano for fun, not to compete or perform. It’s surprisingly enjoyable when you don’t care how good or bad you sound.

Web Design

Web Design fascinates me because it’s a blend of art and tech. Right now, I’m learning the basics of HTML/CSS and I’ll move on to the advanced stuff in the future.

Learning

A big part of improving myself involves learning new things. Currently, I’m taking an online course on programming, and another one on web design

Community Involvement

I like to better the world and those around me, so I’m involved in a lot of organizations revolving my school, including National Honor society, RSD Superintendent’s Advisory Council, and Student Senate.

Leisure

Competitive Smash

The strategy and mechanics of competitive Smash Ultimate is incredibly interesting to me, so I’m hoping to play it competitively. However, I still have a lot of work to go if I want to play on a high level.

Modded Minecraft

This is a great way for me to unwind. Modded Minecraft changes the game completely, adding thousands of new things to do. It’s even better with friends.

Reading

From manga to non-fiction, reading is another way for me to unwind. Right now, I’m reading Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind

Music

In addition to the piano, I love to listen to music. I’d like to think I have tolerant taste, but I mostly listen to pop. If you have any recommendations, send them to me
